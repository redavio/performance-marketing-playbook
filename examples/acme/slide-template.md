# Acme Analytics: slide template

The base deck every presentation uses: the weekly update (if slides are asked for), the monthly report, the QBR, the annual plan and postmortems. The PowerPoint file [slide-template.pptx](slide-template.pptx) has these layouts; it opens in Google Slides. Your AI can also build the deck in Google Slides from this outline through its own connector.

## Rules

- One message per slide, written as the title in a full sentence ("Pipeline is 12% behind plan because LinkedIn CPL doubled"), not a label ("Pipeline").
- Numbers come from the [budget tracker](budget-tracker.csv). Every chart says the period and the source.
- Green, amber, red for status vs plan: green at or above 100% of plan to date, amber 80-99%, red below 80% (rule of thumb).
- No more than 3 bullets per slide. Detail goes in an appendix.

## Layouts

1. **Title:** deck name, Acme Analytics, period, presenter.
2. **Headline:** one sentence and 3-4 large numbers (pipeline vs plan, SAOs, spend, cost per SAO).
3. **Funnel vs plan:** table of MQLs → SQLs → SAOs → closed won with plan, actual, variance and status color.
4. **Trend:** one chart (e.g. pipeline by month vs plan), title states the takeaway.
5. **Drivers:** two columns, "Levers we control" and "Outside forces".
6. **Capture vs creation:** two columns, each with its own measures.
7. **Tests:** table of test, result, decision.
8. **Plan:** goal, budget ($240,000 for the period in this plan), priorities.
9. **Asks and risks.**
10. **Appendix:** detail tables.

Filled for Acme Analytics: pipeline goal $3,000,000, budget $240,000, 100 SAOs needed.
