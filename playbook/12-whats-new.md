# 12. What's new

*Checked November 17, 2026. This changes fast. Re-check every claim against the platform's own docs before you spend on it.*

## Why it matters

New placements are cheap while few advertisers use them, and platform changes can quietly break bidding, tracking or reporting. A simple routine catches both without chasing every headline.

## Ask first

- Does the new placement reach your buyers, at a moment they are looking or open to it?
- Can you measure it the same way as everything else: tracked to SAO, or with a holdout?
- What is the smallest test that would teach you something? ([07-experimentation.md](07-experimentation.md))
- Does a platform change touch your conversions, bidding, targeting or reports?

## Do this

1. **Scan weekly, act monthly.** Read one or two news sources each week (below). Once a month, decide: ignore, watch, or test. Log tests in the [experiment database](../deliverables/experiment-database.csv).
2. **Test new placements small.** Default: a capped budget (rule of thumb: 5 to 10% of the channel it replaces or extends), one offer, one quarter, its own tracking link and a "how did you hear about us" option. Judge it as capture or creation, whichever it is.
3. **Treat platform changes as risks first.** For each change, ask what it does to conversions, bids, targeting and reports. Note the date in your change log, so a later shift in results can be traced.
4. **Check what you can turn off.** Many new placements arrive inside existing campaigns. Find out whether they can be excluded and whether they are reported apart.

**Placements to know, as of November 17, 2026:**

- **ChatGPT ads.** OpenAI shows ads in ChatGPT on the Free and Go plans; Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise plans have no ads ([OpenAI: Our approach to advertising](https://openai.com/index/our-approach-to-advertising-and-expanding-access/)). That leaves out most B2B buyers using ChatGPT at work on a business plan. Advertisers buy through a beta self-serve Ads Manager, paying per thousand impressions or per click, or through agency partners ([OpenAI: New ways to buy ChatGPT ads](https://openai.com/index/new-ways-to-buy-chatgpt-ads)). Treat it as a small capture-style test on clicks.
- **Ads in Google AI Mode and AI Overviews.** Google says existing Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns are eligible to serve there, with no new campaign or budget. You cannot opt out of these placements or target only them, and their results are mixed into standard reports. Exact and phrase match keywords serve only on searches with clear commercial intent; broad match and AI Max also reach exploratory questions ([Google Ads Help: About ads in AI-powered experiences in Google Search](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16297775)). On October 28, 2026, Search Engine Roundtable reported AI Mode ads expanding to ten more countries and showing on follow-up questions in the U.S. ([Search Engine Roundtable](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-expands-ads-ai-mode-42412.html)). Watch your search terms and cost per SAO.
- **Microsoft Copilot.** Microsoft says AI Max for Search delivers ads on Copilot Search and Copilot Answers as well as Bing, and announced it in open pilot from May 2026 ([Microsoft Advertising blog, April 2026](https://about.ads.microsoft.com/en/blog/post/april-2026/win-across-all-three-eras-of-the-web)). Ask your rep whether your Search campaigns can use it.
- **Reddit.** Useful for technical and practitioner buyers who research in communities. Test with subreddit and keyword targeting ([05-demand-creation.md](05-demand-creation.md)).
- **Podcasts and creators.** Host-read podcast ads and creator posts on LinkedIn reach niche B2B audiences. Measure by self-reported source and a dedicated link, over a quarter.
- **AI answers, unpaid.** Being named in AI answers is now a channel of its own ([04-demand-capture.md](04-demand-capture.md), AEO and GEO).

## If you see X, do Y

- **Results shift with no change on your side:** check the news for a platform change that week before touching bids.
- **A new placement turns on inside your campaigns:** find its setting and report; if neither exists, watch search terms and cost per SAO for a month.
- **A vendor pitches a "first mover" placement:** ask how it is measured to SAO, and what a holdout would look like. No answer, no test.
- **A test placement gets clicks but no meetings:** stop at the end of the quarter. Note why in the test log.

## Metrics

- Cost per SAO for each test placement versus the channel it extends.
- Self-reported source mentions of the new placement.
- Before vs after results around each logged platform change.

## Common mistakes

- Chasing every launch with no hypothesis or budget cap.
- Judging a new creation placement on last click in its first month.
- Not logging platform changes, so you can't explain a shift later.
- Trusting third-party blog summaries over the platform's own docs.

## Call an expert when

- A platform change hits conversion tracking or bidding and results move.
- You want to test a placement that needs a holdout to read.
